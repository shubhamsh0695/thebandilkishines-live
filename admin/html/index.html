Branding

Update the storefront banner, logo, or configure the optional opening video. Only the video is shown on the opening screen.

Storefront banner

Use a JPEG or PNG image up to 5 MB. This image appears behind the main storefront banner text.

Store logo

Use a JPEG or PNG image up to 5 MB. The logo appears in the storefront header, footer, and browser tab.

Starting video

Use an MP4 or WebM video up to 25 MB. It plays muted over the intro screen and can be skipped. The logo animation is disabled when no intro video is configured.

Footer links

Change footer section headings, link names and destinations, or add and remove links. Explore links with a category filter open the matching shop category.
